A world worth exploring.

Explore the surface world and use colors to group chunks your way. Pick a color and tap a chunk; tap it with the same color to clear it, or choose another color to replace it.

Highlights save automatically in this browser. Clearing site data removes them, and other browsers or devices have their own highlights. The gold outline marks the last selected chunk; sample-area buttons never change your colors. With the map focused, press Escape to dismiss the outline.
